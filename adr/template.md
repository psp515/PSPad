# ADR-XXXX: <Title>

> Status values: `Proposed` (not yet acted on) · `Active` (in force) ·
> `Superseded by ADR-XXXX` (replaced — link the ADR that replaces it, and add
> `Supersedes ADR-XXXX` to the new one's status line if it fully replaces an
> older decision).

## Context

What forces are at play — the problem, constraint, or question that made a
decision necessary. State it as it looked *at the time*, not with hindsight.

## Decision

What was decided, stated as a firm sentence ("We will ..."), not a
description of options.

## Considered alternatives

- **Option A** — why it was rejected.
- **Option B** — why it was rejected.

Only list alternatives that were genuinely weighed. "We didn't consider X"
is not an alternative.

## Consequences

What this decision makes easier, what it makes harder, and what it costs if
it turns out to be wrong. Include both the positive and the negative —
an ADR with no downsides is missing something.
